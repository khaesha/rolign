# Cover letter rules

Return a concise draft in the selected language for the confirmed company and
job title. Omitted `--language` means English; `--language id` means
Indonesian.

- Use only facts supported by `resume.md`.
- Never invent employers, titles, dates, metrics, skills, certifications, or
  achievements.
- Connect supported experience to the job's stated priorities.
- Mark it as a draft through the saved `cover-letter.md` heading; do not add
  unsupported claims or hiring predictions.
- Preserve company and role names, employers, titles, dates, metrics, skills,
  achievements, resume evidence, and quoted job keywords exactly as supplied.
